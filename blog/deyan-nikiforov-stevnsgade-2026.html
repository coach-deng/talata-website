Deyan is playing 1. division at seventeen

August 2026 · Coach Deng, Talata Basketball

Deyan Yuriev Nikiforov is one of two players still around from the first Talata season. He was in the group of twenty in October 2024, he came all the way through the Academy, and this season he moves up to Stevnsgade in the 1. division.

He is seventeen.

The move

Talata Academy → Stevnsgade, 1. division

  • Founding group of twenty, 2024
  • Academy U19 and Talata Men
  • Steps up at 17

Why this is a bigger jump than it sounds

Danish senior basketball goes Basketligaen, then 1. division, then 2., then 3. Talata Men play 3. Division Herrer Øst. Stevnsgade's 1. division side is two tiers above that, and it is senior basketball, meaning grown men with jobs and ten years of it in their legs.

A seventeen year old holding a spot in that is not the normal Danish youth pathway. The normal pathway is that you play your own age group until you are nineteen and then you find out whether you can handle seniors.

How he got there

By never playing his own age group when he could avoid it. Deyan trained with the U19s and with the Men's side while he was still U17 eligible, which meant most weeks he was one of the least experienced people on the floor.

That is uncomfortable and it is the whole point. You cannot learn to play against bigger, stronger, quicker people by playing against people your own size. It is also why the club runs a senior team at all. A Men's roster gives a seventeen year old somewhere to go on a Wednesday night that is genuinely harder than what he did on Monday.

We are glad he is going

People assume a club wants to keep its best players. What a club should want is for its best players to be somewhere that stretches them, and to know that Talata is where that started.

Deyan gets a harder season than we could give him. Good. That was the job.
