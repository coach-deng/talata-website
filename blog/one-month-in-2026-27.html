Club Update

One month in. Where Talata stands.

9 September 2026 · Coach Deng, Talata Basketball

Season three started on Monday 10 August. One month on, this is where the basketball club on Østerbro stands.

Five years old to adult

Talata runs from five-year-olds at Strandvejsskolen on a Tuesday afternoon to the Men on a Friday night at Nørre Fælled. Same club, same two things we coach everywhere: guard close and take every rebound.

Nine games in Malmö

Two squads crossed the bridge for Malbas Madness and played nine games in three days. Some we won, some we did not, and every player came home with more minutes against real opposition than a month of training gives you.

Talata played Talata

On 4 September we put the Seniors and the Academy on the same floor at Nørre Fælled. Seniors 60, Academy 37. Three days later the same group did this.

Cup night

Talata Men 78, Hørsholm 67. Divisionspokalen 1/16 final, Monday 7 September, Svanemøllehallen. A 3. Division club in its first season in the league system beat a 1. Division side at home. Jordan Naughton 26 points, Deng Awak 19, Valdemar Scharfe 13, and Deyan, 17 years old, 11 points in all 40 minutes. The full story and the box score.

Thirty-one members ran the night as crew, from the U13 kids on the floor to the scorers’ table.

The 1/8 final is away, between 22 and 24 September, against NSBU or BK Amager. Who, when and the road to the final.

Two of ours moved on

Johan was one of the twenty players in Talata’s first season. At fifteen he started at Martins Ferry High School in Ohio. Marius moved up two tiers from Talata Men to Næstved in the 1. Division. Both came through here, and both were helped out of the door. That is what the pathway is for.

This weekend, and the weeks after

BMS Herlev Cup, Friday 11 to Sunday 13 September. Five Talata teams, 16 games: U13 with two squads, U15, U19 and the U17 girls.

First home league game: U19 vs Værløse, Friday 18 September, 19:30, Nørre Fælled Skole.

Men open at home to ALBA on Friday 2 October, 19:30, Nørre Fælled Skole.

Every game, every hall

Every Talata home game is free to watch. Bring people.

What is new

Saturdays. From October, Mini at 10:00 and Junior at 11:00 at Kulturhuset Indre By, built for families where weekday afternoons do not work. Details.

Friday Men and Fitness now train at Nørre Fælled Skole, Biskop Krags Vænge 7, 18:30 to 20:15. Monday stays at Svanemøllehallen.

October camps. Starter Camp for ages 5 to 11 and Academy Camp for ages 12 to 16, Monday 12 to Thursday 15 October at Hafnia-Hallen. Early bird ends Monday 21 September. Camps.

New players are welcome all year

Ages 5 to adult, across Østerbro. Your first session is free. Bring indoor trainers and a water bottle, we bring the balls.
