What is Talata Rookies?

For the 8 to 11-year-olds who are ready to actually play. Real basketball, real game decisions, real teamwork — without the pressure of league commitment yet. Think of it as the bridge between Mini and Academy.

Kids at this age are ready to dribble with both hands, pass under pressure, defend, run plays. Rookies sessions are designed around that growth. Less standing in line, more 3-on-3.

Got a 5 to 7-year-old? Check out Talata Mini — same gym, ages 5-7, runs Mon + Wed (from August).

Real game decisions

3-on-3, 5-on-5, half-court games. Learn by reading the play, not by drilling in lines.

Both hands, every direction

Dribble, pass, shoot with both hands. Defend in all four directions. The foundation Academy is built on.

Compete with respect

Win some, lose some, shake hands after. The mental game starts here.

Same coach as Mini

Coach George leads both Mini and Rookies. So when kids move up, the transition is invisible.

Academy is next

When kids are ready (usually 11-12), they move into Talata Academy. Same club, more commitment, league games.

Boys and girls together

Mixed group at this age. Smaller cohort but everyone plays. Family vibe.

What's next at 12?

When your kid is ready for league basketball (usually around 12), they move up to Talata Academy. Real teams, real games, U13 first, then U15, U17, U19.

Academy plays in the Danish Basketball Federation (DBBF) league with proper game schedules, away travel, and a tighter group. The kids who come up from Rookies already know the system, which makes the jump easy.

The full Talata ladder: Mini (5-7) → Rookies (8-11) → Academy U13/U15/U17/U19 → Talata Men. Same club, same coaches, every step ready when your kid is.

Rookies to Academy: When your child is ready, we'll reach out and set them up on the right Academy team. No tryout, no paperwork — just a conversation.
