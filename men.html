Adults 18+ · Talata Men

Grown men's basketball,
on a real team. Two nights a week.

Talata Men is the club's adult side. One group plays 3. Division Herrer Øst in the Danish federation. The other trains alongside them with no league commitment at all. Same hall, same nights, same coaches.

Two ways to play

Both train in the same slot at Svanemøllehallen, split into two zones. You pick the one that matches how much you want to commit, and you can move between them.

Competitive

The registered team. We play 3. Division Herrer Øst in the DBBF league, plus the Danish Cup. There is a promotion route up to 2. Division through the Final Four. Expect league games most weekends and a tryout to get on the roster.

Fitness

Recreational adult basketball, no DBBF commitment and no weekend games. Turn up, train, play. This is the one for people coming back to the game after a few years off, or starting properly for the first time.

What it costs

2.000 kr per half-season, the same for Competitive and Fitness. That covers the hall, the coaching and your league registration. Pay with MobilePay to 52697 or by bank transfer once you are on the team.

Full price list and the other age groups are on the prices and times page.

Come and train, free

Turn up on a Monday or a Friday at 18:30 with indoor shoes. Email us first so we know to look out for you and can tell you which zone to join.
