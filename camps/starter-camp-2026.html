Starter Camp 2026

Two days of basketball in the autumn break, ages 5 to 12
Oct 12 and 13 · week 42
Ages 5 to 12
Strandvejsskolen · Sionsgade 1
09:30 to 14:30 · 25 places

What it is

Two full days of basketball over efterårsferie for ages 5 to 12. Fundamentals, small sided games and a lot of shots up.

Doors open 09:15 on the Monday and we start at 09:30. Snacks and fruit both days. Bring a packed lunch.

Two proper days with the hall to themselves beats four half days sharing it, which is why the week is split by age.

A day at camp

Morning

Ball handling, footwork and shooting, broken into small groups by age so a seven year old is not guarding a twelve year old.

Middle

Packed lunch together, then games. Small sided, so everyone touches the ball instead of watching the tallest kid.

Afternoon

Competitions, shooting games and a tournament to finish. Home at 14:30.

Why join

Real coaching

The same Talata coaches who run the season, not babysitters with a whistle.

It covers the week

You are back at work and they need somewhere to be. Two days, sorted, 09:00 to 14:00.

No experience needed

Plenty of kids turn up having never played. That is normal and it is the point.

Come back Friday

The older camp plays games at Svanemøllehallen on the Friday from 10:30. You are welcome to come and watch.

Older sibling?

Pathway Camp runs Wednesday to Friday of the same week for ages 13 to 17, at Svanemøllehallen. Three days on what playing in America actually takes, with a measured profile, filmed games and a written evaluation at the end.

Get on the early list

Booking is open. Early bird closes Monday 21 September, after which it goes to 795 kr. 25 places.
