What you do

You get measured, you get filmed, and you get a written evaluation on the last day.

A day at camp

09:00 to 11:30
Testing and skills. Height, wingspan, standing reach, vertical, lane agility and timed shooting
11:30 to 12:30
Lunch, then film. Your own footage from the morning
12:30 to 14:00
Live play, filmed from a fixed camera. Wednesday closes with each player's written evaluation

Questions parents ask

What do they bring?

Indoor shoes and a water bottle. Lunch, snacks and the camp t-shirt come from us.

Drop off and pick up?

Doors 08:45, start 09:00, pick up 14:00, all three days.

Who is it for?

Players aged 13 to 17 who are already in a club and want to know where they actually stand.
