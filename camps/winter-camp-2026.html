Winter Camp 2026

3 days of basketball in the winter school break
Dec 28-30 · Mon-Wed
Ages 8-15 · single track
Svanemøllehallen

The gap week, sorted

School is out. New Year's Eve isn't here yet. Kids are climbing the walls, parents are back at work. We've got 3 days of basketball ready to go.

Mixed group ages 8-15, single track. Good level kids and beginners both welcome, we split into skill groups inside the gym so everyone gets stretched the right amount.

Pricing

Early bird
900 kr
Per player · book before Nov 1
1,100 kr from Nov 1 onwards

Includes: 3 days of coaching, lunch + snacks each day, camp t-shirt

What a day looks like

09:00, 09:30
Drop off + warmup at Svanemøllehallen
09:30, 11:30
Skills block: shooting, dribbling, footwork (split by level)
11:30, 12:30
Lunch + film session (highlights from yesterday's games)
12:30, 14:30
Games + competitive play (3v3, 5v5, tournaments)
14:30, 15:00
Cooldown, day awards, parent pickup

Why it works

Premium gym time

Svanemøllehallen is empty that week. We get the best court at the best time.

Skill jump in 3 days

Intensive small-group coaching. Kids walk out shooting differently than they walked in.

Energy outlet

Holiday boredom is real. Send them home tired. Parents thank us later.

Coach Deng + crew

Same coaches as the season. Not seasonal hires. CLA-based, evidence-based.

Get on the early list

Booking opens November 1. Early list gets first pick of the 24 spots + the 900 kr early bird locked in.
