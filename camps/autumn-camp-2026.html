Efterårsferie · Uge 42

Academy Camp 2026

Three days of basketball in the autumn break, ages 13 to 15
Oct 12, 14 and 15 · week 42
Ages 13 to 15
Hafnia-Hallen · København SV
09:00 to 14:30 · 25 places

What it is

Three full days of basketball over efterårsferie for ages 13 to 15. Proper coaching at game pace, not a holiday club with a ball.

Doors open 08:45 on the Monday and we start at 09:00. Snacks and fruit every day. Bring a packed lunch.

Monday 12, Wednesday 14 and Thursday 15 October. Tuesday is a day off.

Here is what it is not. There are no NCAA coaches in the gym. Nobody is scouting you. Any camp that tells you otherwise is selling you something. This is an honest read from a club that has sent two players down this road.

The three days

Monday · the base

Ball handling under pressure, footwork, and shooting off the catch and off the dribble. The things that decide whether you get minutes.

Wednesday · the game

Reads and decisions at game pace. Pick and roll, closeouts, and defending without fouling. Live play all afternoon.

Thursday · the tournament

A tournament to finish, with families welcome from 12:30. Home at 14:30.

What you get

Real coaching

The same Talata coaches who run the Academy season, in the hall for all three days.

Eight hoops

Hafnia-Hallen is one of the biggest halls in Copenhagen. Nobody queues for a basket, so you get far more shots up than a normal week.

Told where you are

An honest read from the coaching staff at the end of the three days. What is working and what to go and fix.

A team for the week

Most players arrive not knowing everyone. That stops by lunchtime on the Monday.

Why 25 places

Twenty-five is what one hall and this coaching staff can stretch properly. The younger camp runs alongside in the same building, so the club is holding 60 children across the three days and both groups still get a full court.

Younger brother or sister? Starter Camp runs the same three days for ages 5 to 12, in the same hall at the same time. One drop off, one pick up.

Aiming at America? Pathway Camp runs 28 to 30 December for ages 13 to 17. That is the one where you get measured, filmed and evaluated in writing.

Get on the early list

Booking is open. Early bird closes Monday 21 September, after which it goes to 1.495 kr. 25 places.
