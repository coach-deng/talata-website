Autumn Camp 2026

4 days of real basketball during the autumn break
Oct 12-16 · week 42
Starter 5-12 · Mon + Tue · Strandvejsskolen
Pathway 13-17 · Wed to Fri · Svanemøllehallen
09:00 to 14:00 / 14:30 · snacks included

What it is

Structured basketball over efterårsferie, and each age group gets its own days rather than sharing a hall. Junior takes Monday and Tuesday, Academy takes Wednesday to Friday. Doors open 08:45 on your first day and we start at 09:00 every morning. Friday is game day at Svanemøllehallen, first game 10:30 — Academy plays and Junior families are very welcome back to watch. Coaches you already know, gyms you already trust.

Pick your track

🏀 Starter Camp · Ages 5-12

Monday 12 and Tuesday 13 October, 09:00 to 14:00. Strandvejsskolen, Sionsgade 1, 2100 København Ø. Fundamentals, small-sided games, snacks and fruit both days. Bring a packed lunch. Two proper days with the hall to themselves, rather than four half ones sharing it.

695 kr
early bird before Sep 21 · 795 kr standard

🇺🇸 Pathway Camp · Ages 13-17

Wednesday 14 to Friday 16 October, 09:00 to 14:30. Svanemøllehallen, Østerbrogade 240, 2100 København Ø. Shooting machine, live play and film every day. Snacks and fruit provided, bring a packed lunch. Friday is game day.

1,695 kr
early bird before Sep 21 · 1,895 kr standard · 16 places

Why join

Real coaching

Same Talata coaches who run the season. Not babysitters with a whistle. Constraints-led approach.

Holiday-week timing

Parents back at work, kids need a plan. We've got 4 days of energy outlet sorted.

The road to America is real here

Two Talata players are in North America right now. Pathway is three days on what that actually takes, from the coaches who got them there, and you leave with your numbers, your tape and an honest evaluation.

Last day tournament

Friday everyone comes to Svanemøllehallen and plays. First game 10:30. Bring the family, we will be taking photos.

Get on the early list

Booking is open. Early bird closes Monday 21 September, after which Starter goes to 795 kr and Pathway to 1,895 kr. 25 places on Starter, 16 on Pathway.
