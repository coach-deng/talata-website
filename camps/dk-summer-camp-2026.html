The gym

Friday August 7 · Finale

All-Star Game + Family Day

We close out the week with an All-Star Game at Nørre Fælled Skole. All parents, siblings, and Mini players are welcome to come watch. Awards, snacks, and a proper send-off after.

What you get

Training

2 to 3 sessions per day across three courts and eight hoops. Fundamentals, skill stations, and 5-on-5 games. Nobody stands in line

Video analysis

We film and break down games together. Basketball IQ in practice

Shooting machine

Shooting machine access all week. More reps, faster development
