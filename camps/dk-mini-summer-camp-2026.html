All-Star Game + Family Day

Same hall as the older campers, we close the week together with an All-Star Game at Nørre Fælled Skole. All parents, siblings and coaches welcome. Awards, snacks, proper send-off.

What you get

  • 4 days of coached training, fundamentals through games
  • Skill stations: dribbling, passing, shooting
  • Small games and 3v3 / 5v5
  • Snack included every day
  • Friday All-Star Game with the older camp
  • Same venue as the U13+ camp, easy sibling drop-off
  • Big gym: three courts and eight hoops, everyone gets reps
