Who is actually in the gym

Plenty of clubs put a coach's playing career on a page and leave it there. What matters more is who runs the session on a wet Thursday in November. At Talata the head coaches are fixed to a team, and the assistants float, so on any given night your kid is likely to be coached by more than one of these four.

Every one of them has played the game at a level above the one they coach. That is deliberate. They have all been the worst player in a gym at some point, and they remember what it took to stop being that.

Head Coach

Deng Awak

Head of U19 and U11 · floats across every team

Deng founded Talata in 2021 and still coaches four days a week. He played college basketball in Canada and then professionally in Canada, Lithuania and Denmark, and has coached in all three plus Indonesia.

  • Played college basketball in Canada, then pro in Canada, Lithuania and Denmark
  • Has coached in four countries: Canada, Lithuania, Indonesia and Denmark
  • Built and ran the basketball academy at The British School of Vilnius in Lithuania
  • Founded Talata in 2021, from twenty players to over eighty

coach@talatabasketball.dk

A 6'9" forward from Rancho Cucamonga, California. Jordan came up through Etiwanda High School and played NCAA Division 1 at the University of Wyoming in the Mountain West, graduating in 2019, before turning professional in Denmark with BK Amager in the Basketligaen.

  • NCAA Division 1, University of Wyoming, Mountain West Conference
  • Professional in Denmark with BK Amager, Basketligaen
  • 6'9" forward, Etiwanda High School, California

Lead Coach · Mini and Junior

Georg Jespersen

Head coach of U13 and U9 · everyone calls him George

George runs the youngest end of the club. Mini, Junior and up through U15, which means he is the coach most Talata families meet first. He played his own youth basketball at Falcon here in Copenhagen, so he has been the eight year old in the gym as well as the one running it.

He is studying sustainability at Copenhagen Business School alongside the coaching. He is also the one who has already watched the tape twice before anyone asks.

  • Played his youth basketball at Falcon, Copenhagen
  • Head coach, U13 and U9, both in Grand Prix Øst
  • Lead coach for Mini (5 to 9) and Junior (10 to 12) at Strandvejsskolen
  • Studying sustainability at Copenhagen Business School
  • Film study, defensive systems and game prep across the youth teams

How we coach

We do not run drills in lines.

Talata coaches on the constraints-led approach. Players learn inside games with the rules bent to force a skill, rather than standing in a queue repeating a movement nobody has to make under pressure. It is why our sessions look louder and messier than you might expect, and why the kids keep turning up.
