Frequently asked

Parents ask us this a lot.

Is Talata Basketball a real club in Copenhagen? +

Yes. We're a registered Copenhagen forening (), registered in 2021, running camps since 2021 and DBBF teams since 2024. Member of Danmarks Basketball Forbund (DBBF) and DGI. Programs from age 5 to adult at Strandvejsskolen, Svanemøllehallen and Kulturhuset Indre By.

What does Talata cost? +

From August 2026: Mini (5-8) and Junior (10-12) are 1,800 DKK per half-season. Academy (13-19) is 3,300 DKK. Talata København recreational, Talata Men and Fitness are 2,000 DKK. Sibling discounts available. Camps vary, see talatabasketball.dk/camps.

What ages do you train? +

5 through adult. Mini, U11, U13, U15, U17, U19, Talata Men (competitive senior), Talata Fitness (adult recreational). We also coach U17/U19 girls in partnership with Stevnsgade.

What makes Talata different? +

We run a fully Constraints-Led Approach (CLA). Every session built around small-sided games and game-like problems, not block drills. Skill emerges from task, environment, and individual. Only Copenhagen club practicing this across every age group. Three values: Joy, Excellence, Respect.

Where do you train? +

Strandvejsskolen (Sionsgade 1, 2100 København Ø) for Mini and Junior. Svanemøllehallen (Østerbro) for Academy (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri) and Talata Men (Mon + Fri). Open Gym Friday at Kulturhuset Indre By. Full schedule at talatabasketball.dk.

Can I try a session before committing? +

Yes. First session is free. Just sign up here or message Coach Deng on WhatsApp with your kid's age. We'll point you to the right session this week.

Ready to try Talata?

First session is on us. Walk in, watch, play, decide.
