Free · from Coach Deng

The 4-week home program

The same work Coach Deng gives his own players. 20 minutes, 4 days a week. A ball, a wall and a bit of space. For players 10 and up.

Get the program →

Free PDF, 6 pages. Print it or keep it on your phone.

4 weeks16 sessions
20 minper session
1 balland a wall

How it works

When

4 days a week. Pick days you can keep. On game days, do the ball block only.

How long

20 minutes, about 28 in week 4. Same four blocks every time.

What you need

A basketball, a wall and a bit of space. A hoop helps, not needed.

The goal

Strong ankles, knees and hips, and the ball in your hands most days.

Every session

  • 3 minWarm-up. Get loose and land soft.
  • 8 minBody. Core, hips and calves. Three seconds on the way down.
  • 7 minBall. Dribbling, wall passes and form shots.
  • 2 minCool down. Calf, hamstring and hip stretches.

Every week adds a little. The last page is a tracker with 16 boxes. Tick one after every session.

Safety first. Warm up every time. Sore muscles in week 1 are normal. Sharp pain in a heel, a knee or your back means stop that move and tell a parent or a coach.

Finished all 16? Tell us at coach@talatabasketball.dk. We want to hear how it went.
