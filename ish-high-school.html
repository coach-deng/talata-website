More than basketball

At Talata Basketball, High School students from ISH, the International School of Hellerup, do not just play basketball. They become part of something bigger. Training alongside players from Talata Basketball, you step into an international environment where different backgrounds, languages and stories come together on the same court. The language on the floor is English, so you will feel at home from day one.

The group takes the game seriously and takes each other seriously. Players push each other in training and back each other up when it matters. That is most of what makes a season worth turning up for.

Every coach here has played professionally, and it shows in how the sessions are built. Whether you are chasing something competitive or just want to get better, the training is the same standard.

Want more? If you are hungry to train more days a week, that door is wide open too.

International by default

Players from all over the world on the same team. English is the working language, on and off the court.

Coaches who played

Every coach has played professionally and coaches from that.

Room to go further

Start with one session a week. Add more days whenever you are ready.
