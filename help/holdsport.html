Guide

Holdsport, in English

The app is Danish only, even on holdsport.com. That is the main problem, and it is not you.

← All guides

Holdsport is how we run sign-ups, cancellations, the calendar and payments. Every Danish club uses it or something like it. The catch is that it has no English version, so here is what each button actually means.

Read this before you click join

Joining a team in Holdsport starts your membership fee automatically. You do not need it to come and try. A free trial session needs nothing at all, just turn up. Only join a team once you have decided to stay.

Creating the profile

The one mistake nearly everyone makes

Create the profile in your child's name, not your own. The invitation screen offers to attach it to an existing profile, and it is very easy to attach your child's membership to yourself. Parents get added to the child afterwards, so start with the player.

You will be asked for: first name, last name, email, mobile, street, postcode, city, date of birth, a username and a password, plus three tick boxes for terms and data consent. Have those ready and it takes about three minutes.

Signing up and cancelling

Please do both. Tilmeld when your child is coming and Afmeld when they are not.

Coaches plan the session on those numbers, how many stations, how many teams, how much ball each kid gets. Eight players and fourteen players are two different sessions.

Use the app, not the website

Once the profile exists, the phone app is much easier than the website. Search Holdsport on the App Store or Google Play. Same login.

Payments

Your invoice lives under Betalinger. If you were charged twice, or never got an invoice at all, come and talk to us. We will sort it, and you will not be chased for something you do not owe.

Anything still unclear, email coach@talatabasketball.dk.
