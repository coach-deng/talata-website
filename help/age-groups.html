If your child is 12

Twelve sits on the line. Junior is the default and it is the right place to start. If they want more court time and a competitive team, Academy is open to them instead. Talk to Deng and we will work out which one fits, it is not a decision you have to make from a table.

Sibling arrangements and financial support both exist. If money is the reason your child is not here, come and talk to Deng before you walk away. That conversation is private and it happens more often than you would think.

Why a 15-year-old plays U17

The groups above are about training. Match teams work differently. The Danish federation sets them by how old your child is on 1 October, and you play UXX if you are still under XX on that date.

So a player who turns 17 after 1 October is still 16 on the day that counts, and plays U17 all season. In practice, a 15-year-old is usually U17, not U15.

If your child's birthday sits near the cutoff, ask us rather than working it out. Two kids born in the same year can end up on different teams, and we would rather tell you than have you guess.

Changing group

Kids move up when they are ready, not when a birthday says so. Deng has the final call on playing up an age, and several of our players train across two groups. Ask.

Full detail on the season is in the 2026/27 Parents' Guide.
