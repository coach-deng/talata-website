Girls 6-14 · Talata Sparks

Basketball, just for
the girls. And it's hers.

Talata Sparks is our girls only programme for ages 5 to 11. Mondays at Strandvejsskolen, coached by Deng with our older girls helping out. Whether she has held a ball before or not, this is where she starts.

What is Talata Sparks?

Sparks is part of Talata Girls. The younger ones get a gym that belongs to them for an hour, and it changes how they play. Nobody hides at the back and nobody waits around for a turn.

Our U17 and U19 girls, the Big Sisters, help out every session. Every Spark has an older girl in her corner from day one.

Girls only

An hour that belongs to them. Confidence comes easier when it is just the girls.

Coach Deng + Big Sisters

Our U17 and U19 girls help coach. Instant role models, instant belonging.

Movement first

She will be moving almost the whole hour. Playing, not queuing.

Ball control

The basics, pitched at what a 6 year old can actually manage.

First time? Perfect.

Most girls who join have never played before. That is exactly who this is for.

We know her name

Small group, so the coaches know every girl and how she learns.

Meet the Big Sisters

Our older girls, the U17s and U19s, lead the Sparks sessions. They have been exactly where the little ones are now.

A teenager who plays the game and is on your side changes what a 7 year old thinks is possible. The older girls get to lead, which they take seriously. This is the main reason girls stay with us.

Training schedule

We train Mondays at Strandvejsskolen. One hour, all girls, all season. Come every week, it is the same time and place.

What's next as she grows?

As she gets older, she keeps moving up through Talata Girls, into our U13 and U15 teams, and on to the U17 and U19 girls.

Same club and the same coaches the whole way up. One day the Big Sisters she looked up to are just her teammates.
