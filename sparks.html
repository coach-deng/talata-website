What is Talata Sparks?

Talata Sparks is part of Talata Girls, our home for girls in the game. The little ones play, learn and build confidence in a room that is all theirs. No standing at the back, no waiting for a turn. Every girl gets the ball.

Our older players, the Big Sisters, help out every session. So every Spark has someone who looks like her, plays like her, and believes in her, from day one.

Girls only

A room that is all theirs. Confidence comes easier when it is just the girls.

Coach Deng + Big Sisters

Our U17 and U19 girls help coach. Instant role models, instant belonging.

Movement first

Running, jumping, games. Every session is built around playing, not standing in lines.

Ball control

Dribbling, passing, shooting. Broken down to what a 6-year-old can actually do.

First time? Perfect.

Most girls who join have never played before. That is exactly who this is for.

We know her name

Small group. Coaches know every girl, how she learns, what she needs.

Meet the Big Sisters

Our older girls, the U17s and U19s, lead the Sparks sessions. They have been exactly where the little ones are now.

Having a teenager who looks like you, plays like you, and is in your corner changes everything. The Sparks get role models. The Big Sisters get to lead. That is the Talata Girls difference, and it is why girls stay.

What's next as she grows?

As she gets older, she keeps moving up through Talata Girls, into our U13 and U15 teams, and on to the U17 and U19 girls.

Same club, same coaches, all the way up. And one day the Big Sisters she looked up to become her teammates.
