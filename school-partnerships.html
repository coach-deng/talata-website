Why schools work with us

Plenty of children would love basketball and never get near it. The club is across town and training starts before a parent can get off work. So the sport just stays out of reach.

So we go the other way. We come to the school. Our coaches collect the children from the school door, run the session, and bring them back. There is nothing for a parent to organise and nothing for the school to staff.

The children get exactly the same coaching our club players get, from people who have played the game at a serious level. We are not sending someone to keep them busy for an hour.

We handle the logistics

Pickup from school, session, return to school. The school does not need to staff it.

Real coaching

The same coaches and the same method we use with our own club teams.

A path onwards

Children who catch the bug can carry straight on into our club teams and camps.

What happened at Strandvejsskolen

Forty children, and a waiting list.

When we started running sessions at Strandvejsskolen in Østerbro, the program filled to forty children with five more waiting. Over the same spring our under-13 group went from a handful of players to more than thirty. That is what happens when the barrier to trying is removed.

Schools we work with

Since 2025 · Østerbro

Strandvejsskolen

Strandvejsskolen is the home of our Mini and Junior programs. Training runs in the school gym at Sionsgade 1, so the children never leave the building they already know.

We also run sessions with the school's SFO, and our coaches collect the children directly from SFO before training. It is the arrangement that proved the model works.

ISH, International School of Hellerup

ISH High School trains Thursday evenings at Svanemøllehallen alongside our own U17 and U19 squads. English on the floor, professional coaches, and the option to add more training days for anyone who wants them.

Our ISH Primary School programme is being re-planned for 26/27 and we will publish the new day and venue once it is locked. Email coach@talatabasketball.dk to be told first.

DGI

Our school trainings are run in collaboration with DGI, whose support is what lets us put qualified coaches into schools rather than asking the schools to fund it themselves.

How a partnership starts

1
You get in touchAn email is enough. Tell us the school, the ages, and roughly how many children.
2
We come and see the spaceWe look at the gym or hall, and work out what is realistic on your timetable.
3
We agree a day, a time and a coachIncluding how pickup and return will work, so parents have nothing to arrange.
4
We run a tasterThe children try it before anyone commits to a season.
5
The program runsWeekly sessions, and a route into the club for anyone who wants to keep going.
