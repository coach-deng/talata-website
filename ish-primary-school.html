ISH Primary School · Talata Basketball

Basketball should be fun.
First, second, and always.

An hour of real basketball every Friday for children at ISH, the International School of Hellerup. We collect your child from school and bring them back afterwards. No extra logistics for you.

Fun first, and it shows

Our skilled coaches bring years of coaching experience and a genuine love for the game, and that joy is contagious. Your child will feel it from the very first practice.

Our coaches take a real interest in every single player. They learn every child's name, cheer them on, and keep the energy high with playful drills and friendly little competitions that make the kids want to come back for more.

Training is planned so both first-timers and more experienced players are challenged at just the right level. Everyone gets to grow their game, at their own pace, in a space where trying, and even missing, is part of the fun.

Every child by name

Small enough that no one gets lost, and every player gets coached.

Right level for everyone

Complete beginners and kids who already play, challenged side by side.

No logistics for you

We handle pickup and return. You just collect a happy, tired child at the normal time.

How the afternoon works

To make things easy for busy families, your child's basketball coach handles the whole journey.
