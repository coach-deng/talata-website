Every home game is free.

Talata plays in the Danish federation leagues from U13 up to the men's 3. Division, plus friendlies and tournaments at home and abroad. You do not need a ticket, a membership or a reason. Turn up.

The home night

Talata Night

Ten Fridays this season, one hall, one tip-off time. Nørre Fælled Skole, 19:30, from September to March. Men's 3. Division and U19 1. Division.

Same night every time, so you do not have to check a schedule to know where the club is.

  • Doors from 19:00, tip-off 19:30
  • Nørre Fælled Skole, 2200 København N
  • Free entry, no ticket, bring whoever you like
  • First one: Fri 18 September, U19 vs Værløse

Turn up, get in the draw

Every home game you come to puts you in that month's draw. We pull a name on the last Friday of the month and hand it over at the next game.

  • Talata game jerseys, shooting shirts and training gear
  • A free place at a Talata camp
  • A month of sessions for a new player

You are in. See you at the next home game.

One entry per person per month. Over 18s only, or a parent enters on a family's behalf. We use your email for game reminders and the draw, nothing else, and every mail has an unsubscribe link.

The whole season

League and cup fixtures come from the Danish federation. Friendlies and tournaments come from the club calendar and update on their own. Where a game has no time yet, it says so rather than guessing.

Why some games say Time TBC. The federation sets the date first and the time later, once both clubs and the hall agree. A date with no time is a real date. We publish it as soon as it is set rather than putting a guess in front of you and moving it later.
