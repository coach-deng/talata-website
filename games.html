Talata Night

Claim a ticket, get in the draw

Tickets are free. Claiming one tells us how many to expect and puts you in that month’s draw. We pull a name on the last Friday of the month and hand it over at the next game.

  • Talata game jerseys, shooting shirts and training gear
  • A free place at a Talata camp
  • A 1:1 private session with Coach Deng or Coach Jordan, worth 1.400 kr

Anyone can enter. Under 13s, ask a parent to use their email. Claim a ticket on any home game below.
