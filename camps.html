Talata Basketball runs basketball camps in Copenhagen and abroad for players aged 5 to 17. Summer Camp runs week 32 at Nørre Fælled Skole in two tracks: ages 5-12 (Tue-Fri, Aug 4 to 7) and ages 13-17 (Mon-Fri, Aug 3 to 7), both closing with a joint All-Star Game on Friday Aug 7 with all parents invited. Autumn Camp (Oct 12-15) and Winter Camp (Dec 28-30) are open for the early list. International options include a travel camp to Canada (June, Lethbridge). Every camp is led by Talata coaches with real competitive experience. Registration is open now.
