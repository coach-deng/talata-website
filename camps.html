Talata Basketball runs basketball camps in Copenhagen and abroad for players aged 5 to 17. Summer Camp runs week 32 at Svanemøllehallen in two tracks: ages 8-12 (Tue-Fri, Aug 4 to 7) and ages 13-17 (Mon-Fri, Aug 3 to 7), both closing with a joint All-Star Game on Friday Aug 7 with all parents invited. International options include travel camps to Canada (June, Lethbridge) and Nida, Lithuania (July, Baltic coast). Every camp is led by Talata coaches with real competitive experience.
