Talata Basketball runs basketball camps in Copenhagen and abroad for players aged 5 to 17. Summer Camp Mini (ages 5-10) runs week 32 Tue-Fri (Aug 4 to 7) at Strandvejsskolen. Summer Camp Academy (ages 11-15) runs week 32 Mon-Fri (Aug 3 to 7) at Svanemøllehallen and closes with an All-Star Game on Friday Aug 7 with all parents invited. International options include travel camps to Canada (June, Lethbridge) and Nida, Lithuania (July, Baltic coast). Every camp is led by Talata coaches with real competitive experience.
