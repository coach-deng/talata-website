New for 2026/27

Saturday mornings

Basketball for ages 5 to 12, in the middle of town
Saturdays · all season
Mini 5 to 9 · 10:00 to 11:00
Junior 10 to 12 · 11:00 to 12:00
Kulturhuset Indre By

Why we added it

Our weekday sessions start at 15:30. If both parents work, that is not a session, it is a problem. Enough families told us so that we stopped explaining it and moved a session instead.

Saturday mornings, right in the centre of town. No rush from school, no leaving work early, and you are done before lunch.

The two hours

10:00 to 11:00 · Mini, ages 5 to 9

Fundamentals, small sided games and a lot of shots up. No experience needed, and most of them have never played before.

11:00 to 12:00 · Junior, ages 10 to 12

Real basketball foundations and game decisions. This is the bridge into the Academy.

Two kids, one trip. The groups run back to back on purpose, so a family with a seven year old and an eleven year old makes one journey and waits one hour, not two.

Open Gym follows at 12:00 for anyone who wants to stay on and keep playing.

Who runs it

George and Tyler take the Saturday groups. They are the same pair who run Mini and Junior in the week, so the kids already know them and nothing about the coaching changes because the day does.

Coach Deng drops in on sessions through the season.

Meet the coaches

Where

Kulturhuset Indre By
Charlotte Ammundsens Plads 3, 1359 København K

Central, easy on public transport, and a short walk from Nørreport.

What to bring

Indoor shoes and a water bottle. That is it. We bring the balls. The first session is free and there is nothing to sign up for before you come.

Starting this autumn.

Saturdays begin closer to October and we will confirm the first date shortly. If weekday afternoons do not work for your family, tell us now and you will be the first to know when it starts.

Keep me posted

Already know you want a spot? The first session is free either way, and you can also book a weekday trial in the meantime.
